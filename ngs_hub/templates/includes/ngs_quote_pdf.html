Delivery Instruction

Please mail your samples to the following address: Jiaqi Zhang, Athenomics, 9096169589, 1 Grant St Suite 230, Framingham, MA, 01702 and fill the transportation company and tracking number in the Sample Registration Form, which will be provided later.

Sample Requirement

Species Requirement: if mRNA is the analysis target, common animal or plant can be accepted. If total RNA is the target, only human sample can be accepted at the current stage.

Body Fluid Sample: Recommended Requirement: 100uL - 2.5mL. If to be mailed, mix with RNA Protect Reagent, ship at 4 degree and the expected delivery time should be within 2 days. Drop off samples are recommended to be mixed with RNA Protect Reagent, stored at 4 degree and processed immediately before drop off.

Cell Sample: Recommended Requirement: 1E4 - 1E7 cells. If to be mailed, preserved with cell freezing media, ship with dry ice and the expected delivery time should be within 1 day. Drop off samples are recommended to be stored at 4 degree and processed immediately before drop off.

Tissue Sample: Recommended Requirement: 10mg - 100mg. If to be mailed, ship with dry ice and the expected delivery time should be within 1 day. Drop off samples are recommended to be stored in dry ice and processed immediately before drop off.

Post-Project Sample Handling

The original samples and intermediate samples will be preserved 1 month after the analysis results are delivered and will be discarded by default afterward. If returning or extended storage are required, please specify the requirements in the Sample Registration Form. The data will be preserved 2 months after the analysis results are delivered.
